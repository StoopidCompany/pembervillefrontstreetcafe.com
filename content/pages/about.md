Front Street Cafe opened its doors in downtown Pemberville, Ohio on September 27, 2023, in a charming historic brick building on Front Street. We started this cafe because we believe food is medicine — and food loaded with additives and shelf stabilizers is the opposite. We wanted to build a place where you know exactly what you’re eating, made from ingredients you can trust, prepared where you can see it happen.

That’s what “true scratch kitchen” means to us — not a marketing phrase, but a promise. We bake our own bread, churn our own butter, and build every dish from real ingredients, with a nut-oil free kitchen from top to bottom.

Our menu reflects that commitment, from hand-cut fries fried in beef tallow to scratch-made soups like our House Loaded Potato Soup and French Onion. Guests keep coming back for the Italian Grinder on our house-made hoagie bun, the Fettuccine Alfredo, and our 10 oz Ribeye and Peppercorn Pork Chop from the bar. Ask your server what our chef is featuring this week — our rotating specials highlight the best of what’s in season.

We’re proud to work alongside great local partners who help us serve great food every day, and even prouder to be part of the Pemberville community. Whether you’re stopping in for breakfast, settling in at the bar for dinner, or grabbing something to go, we’re here to serve real food, made with love.
