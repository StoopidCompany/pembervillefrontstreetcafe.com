We want the menu, the hours and the phone number to be usable by everyone, on any device.
Here is what has actually been done, rather than a promise.

## What the site does

- **Every colour pairing has been measured** against the WCAG AA contrast standard, not
  eyeballed. Body text sits at about 15:1 against the background; the lightest text we use
  is 5.8:1, where the standard asks for 4.5:1.
- **Keyboard focus is always visible** — a brick-coloured outline on whatever you have
  tabbed to — and there is a "skip to content" link as the first thing on every page.
- **Every photograph has a description** for screen readers, including the guest photos on
  the gallery and the dishes on the menu.
- **Images reserve their space** before they load, so the page does not jump while you are
  reading it.
- **Nothing important is colour alone.** Star ratings are also written out for screen
  readers, and prices are text rather than pictures.
- **Animation respects your settings.** If your device asks for reduced motion, the site
  stops animating.
- **The order menu works without JavaScript** and can be opened from the keyboard.
- Text can be enlarged with the browser's own zoom without the layout breaking.

## Where we know it falls short

- The map on the contact page is Google's, and we do not control how accessible it is. The
  full address and the drive times are written out as text beside it for that reason.
- Guest reviews are quoted exactly as people wrote them, so some contain typos or missing
  punctuation that a screen reader will read literally. Correcting someone's review felt
  worse than leaving it.

## Telling us about a problem

If something on this site does not work for you, email
[frontstreetcafe16@gmail.com](mailto:frontstreetcafe16@gmail.com) or call the cafe. Say which
page and what happened, and we will fix it.

If it is the building rather than the website — getting in, the tables, the bathroom — call
and ask. It is a historic building and it is better to tell you honestly over the phone than
to guess here.
