Plain English, and short, because this is a cafe website and there is not much to tell.

## What we collect ourselves

Nothing. There is no account to create, no newsletter sign-up, and no cookie set by this
site itself.

## When you send us a message

The form on the [contact page](/contact) passes what you type — your name, email, and
whichever of phone, date, number of people and message you fill in — to a service called
Web3Forms, which emails it to the cafe. We use it to reply to you, and we do not add you to
any list. Web3Forms processes the message on our behalf; their own privacy terms are at
[web3forms.com](https://web3forms.com).

If you would rather not use a form, the phone number is on every page.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see roughly how many people visit and which pages they read —
whether anyone opens the menu, mostly. It sets cookies in your browser and sends Google a
record of the pages you view, including your rough location and the kind of device you are
using. We do not see your name or email, and we do not use it for advertising.

You can block it with any browser setting or extension that blocks analytics, and the site
works exactly the same.

## Other companies your browser talks to

A few parts of the site are served by other people, which means your browser connects to
them when the page loads:

- **Google Maps**, for the map on the contact page.
- **Google**, for the small reviewer photos beside guest reviews, which are hosted on
  Google's servers because the reviews come from there.
- **DoorDash and the other delivery apps**, but only if you click one of those links.

The fonts and all of the photographs are served from this site rather than from anyone
else's, so those do not involve a third party.

## Children

Nothing here is aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything about them.
